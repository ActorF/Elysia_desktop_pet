## 新增与改进

- **36 句日常语音：** 覆盖早晚问候、饭点、休息、欢迎回来、生日和告别等 12 类场景，按本地时间与活跃状态轮换播放。
- **18 句点击互动语音：** 轻点头部摸头、轻点身体戳戳；两处共用计数，3 秒内第 5 次点击触发生气回应，结束后恢复原表情。拖动与缩放不计为点击。
- **完整台词气泡：** 粉边白底，按台词长度调整，最多两行；自动选择人物旁的合适一侧，跟随拖动和缩放。
- **语音协调：** 快速点击合并回应，日常问候与互动录音不会叠加。勿扰或静音时仍可显示互动表情与文字，恢复发声后不补播旧互动。
- **互动与语音设置：** 新增两页设置，分别控制点击互动、鼠标流光和自动问候，并提供勿扰、音量与生日月日选项；升级保留已有位置、大小、装扮和偏好。
- **三种鼠标流光：** 提供粉色流光、紫粉绸光和星尘尾迹，不挡点击、不抢焦点。轨迹保留时间支持 0.1–5 秒的小数，默认 0.6 秒。
- **流光连贯性改进：** 修复首次快速移动时轨迹丢失，改善快速甩动、画圈与多显示器表现；跨屏或锁屏、休眠恢复后重新起笔，避免连接旧轨迹。

## English summary

Version 1.3.0 adds daily voices, click responses, and cursor trails while keeping expressions, outfits, actions, key clouds, dragging, and resizing. This historical stable version remains available; it does not include chat or reply read-aloud.

[Download Windows portable 1.3.0](https://github.com/ActorF/Elysia_desktop_pet/releases/download/v1.3.0/Elysia_desktop_pet-1.3.0.exe) · [GitHub release](https://github.com/ActorF/Elysia_desktop_pet/releases/tag/v1.3.0) · [SHA256](https://github.com/ActorF/Elysia_desktop_pet/releases/download/v1.3.0/Elysia_desktop_pet-1.3.0.exe.sha256)

- **36 daily voice lines:** rotate across 12 situations, including morning/evening greetings, meals, breaks, returning, birthdays, and farewells, based on local time and activity.
- **18 click responses:** head pats and body pokes share a counter; the fifth click within three seconds triggers an annoyed reaction, then restores the previous expression. Dragging and resizing do not count as clicks.
- **Complete speech bubbles:** pink-bordered white bubbles fit the text in up to two lines, choose a suitable side of the character, and follow movement and resizing.
- **Coordinated playback:** rapid clicks are combined, and daily and interaction recordings do not overlap. Do not disturb and mute still allow interaction expressions and text; old interactions are not replayed when sound resumes.
- **Interaction and Voice settings:** two new tabs independently control click responses, cursor trails, and automatic greetings, with Do not disturb, volume, and birthday month/day options. Upgrades retain position, size, outfits, and preferences.
- **Three cursor trails:** Pink glow, Purple-pink ribbon, and Stardust remain click-through and do not take focus. Trail duration accepts decimal values from 0.1 to 5 seconds, defaulting to 0.6 seconds.
- **Smoother trails:** fixes lost trails on the first fast movement and improves flicks, circles, and multiple displays. Changing displays or returning from lock/sleep starts a new path without connecting to old positions.
